# The Safe-Harbor Wallet
**Self-Custody Without the Stress for the Next Billion Users**

---

## 🛑 The Problem
**Web3 is terrifying for regular people.**
- **Complexity:** Seed phrases, gas fees, and hex addresses confuse everyday users.
- **Scams & Phishing:** Billions are lost to malicious smart contracts and phishing links. Users are paralyzed by the fear of making a single irreversible mistake.
- **Centralized Exchange Trap:** Due to fear, users leave funds on exchanges (like FTX), risking total loss of their assets.

---

## 💡 The Solution
**An AI-Protected, Non-Custodial Wallet**
We provide raw blockchain power wrapped in a bank-grade safety net. Our wallet translates complex smart contract interactions into plain English and blocks malicious transactions before they are signed.
- **Self-Custody:** You hold the keys. We don't.
- **Anti-Scam Engine:** Real-time analysis of every transaction and connection.
- **Plain English UX:** No more confusing hex codes. Know exactly what you are approving.

- **Multi-Chain Support:** Ethereum, Binance Smart Chain, Polygon, and Bitcoin integration.
